About

GDM-filled layers are data-driven bioregions from Ward/Hellinger clustering of 2025 iNaturalist plant-genus + animal-species composition on well-sampled 50 km cells, extrapolated to unsampled cells with a Generalized Dissimilarity Model (NPP mean/seasonality + geographic distance).

Official layers are the National Ecological Framework for Canada boundaries at the matching hierarchy level.

Colours are matched between estimated and official layers via optimal bipartite assignment (Hungarian algorithm) on cluster × region cell overlap, so corresponding regions share a colour where possible.
